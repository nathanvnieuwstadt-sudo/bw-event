# BW Event — Banquet Management Platform

## Product purpose
Internal operations tool for restaurant staff to manage banquet bookings, kitchen coordination, and client contacts. Single restaurant, role-based access. Built to scale to multi-tenant SaaS later.

## Register
product

## Users
- **Floor Manager** — primary daily user. Creates and edits banquets, coordinates between client and kitchen. Speed matters; often used mid-service.
- **General Manager** — oversight and approval. Reviews reports, confirms bookings, manages agent settings.
- **Kitchen** — read-only view of upcoming events and menu items. Used on a kitchen screen, possibly on a tablet. Scannable at a glance.
- **Owner** — read-only dashboard. Wants to see status at a glance without operational detail.
- **DEV** — superadmin. Unrestricted.

## Scene
Floor manager at a Belgian restaurant, standing at the front-desk or moving through the room during evening service. Checks banquet details on a mounted 13-inch laptop. Room is dim, warm ambient light. Needs to find information in seconds, not minutes.

## Brand tone
Professional, unhurried, precise. Not corporate-cold. Not trendy-startup. Like a well-run restaurant: everything in its place, nothing flashy, quality in the details.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards with gradient hero numbers
- Loud startup branding with bright gradient accents
- Healthcare or hospitality beige
- Dark "hacker" terminal aesthetics

## Strategic principles
- Clarity first: every screen should answer the user's question before they have to hunt
- Density is a feature for power users, not a problem to solve
- Consistency across screens so users navigate by muscle memory
